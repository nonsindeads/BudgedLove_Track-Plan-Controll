Workflow in one glance

Planung bleibt das Zentrum, Buchungen bestaetigen die Realitaet.

01 · Plan

Recurring payments create the monthly plan. Optional items can be skipped, mandatory ones stay visible.

02 · Import

Bank statements add real bookings. Open items can be assigned before they become final.

03 · Confirm

Actual transactions close the loop, update the forecast, and keep balances accurate.

04 · Review

Dashboards show what is open, overdue, or at risk for the current month.
